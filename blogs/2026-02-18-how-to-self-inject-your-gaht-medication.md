Starting gender-affirming hormone therapy (GAHT) can come with a lot of emotions—excitement, anticipation, and maybe even some nerves. Injectable forms of GAHT (also called HRT) are available for both testosterone and estrogen. If self-injection is part of your journey, it’s completely normal to feel a little intimidated at first.
