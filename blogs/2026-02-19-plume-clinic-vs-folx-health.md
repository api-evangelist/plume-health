Plume Clinic and FOLX both offer gender-affirming care for transgender and gender-diverse adults, including telehealth access and licensed clinicians. For many patients, either provider could meet their basic needs. The key difference isn’t access – it’s how care is structured, how often you’re supported, and how predictable your costs are over time.
